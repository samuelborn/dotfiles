# Rewrite

1. List the facts in the original, without its wording.
2. Decide who reads the text and what they need from it.
3. Order the facts by what matters most to that reader.
4. Write a new text from that list. Keep the original's language and tone, but none of its sentences.
5. Apply the unslop skill.

Ask the user before writing if the reader, the goal, or a fact is unclear.
If the reader needs something the original lacks, like a ticket number or a link, ask for it instead of guessing.
